Counter-pressing and the first forward pass

Tonight's session

75 minutes / Pitch two / Open a drill for coaching detail

01 ARRIVE + ACTIVATE / 15 min

Ball each. Scan before receiving. Progress from unopposed movement to pairs applying light pressure.

  • Open your body before the ball arrives.
  • Coach the first touch, not just the speed.
02 WIN IT BACK / 25 min

Four against two in a tight square. On a turnover, the nearest player presses and the second player closes the forward lane.

Success measure: regain possession within five seconds.
03 TRANSFER INTO THE GAME / 35 min

Seven against seven. Award a bonus point for a goal scored within ten seconds of a regain. Freeze sparingly and let players solve the problem.
